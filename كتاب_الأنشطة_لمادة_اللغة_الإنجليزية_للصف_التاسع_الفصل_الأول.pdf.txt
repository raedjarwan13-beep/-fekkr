ACTIVE PRONUNCIATION | Hard and soft c 
The letter c can be pronounced as /k/ or /s/.
• It is pronounced /k/ when it is followed by 
a, o, u or a consonant (e.g. car, crash).
• It is pronounced /s/ when it is followed by e, 
i or y (e.g. city).
• It is also pronounced /k/ when it appears at 
the end of a word (e.g. athletic, Olympic).
